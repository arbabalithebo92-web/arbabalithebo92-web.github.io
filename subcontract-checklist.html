10 checks before you sign a UK subcontract

A free checklist for subbies. Most final account arguments start with a clause nobody read on day one.

1When exactly do I get paid?

Find the due date and the final date for payment for each application. Diarise them. If they aren't stated, ask before you sign.
Ask: what is the final date for payment after each due date?

2How late can they send a Pay Less Notice?

The contract sets the deadline. A notice that arrives late can't reduce what's owed, so this date is your leverage.
Ask: how many days before the final date must a Pay Less Notice be issued?

3Retention: how much, and when does it come back?

Check the % and the release trigger for each half: your completion or the whole project's, and the end of the defects period. Projects run late, and so does your retention.
Ask: is release tied to my completion or the main contract's?

4Who can actually instruct extras?

Most subcontracts name one person. A foreman's "crack on" may not count. Get the name in writing.
Ask: who is authorised to issue variation instructions?

5How fast must I notify a variation or delay?

Some contracts give 7 days or less. Miss it and you may lose the claim.
Ask: what is the notice period, and who do notices go to?

6Contra charges and set-off

Look for clauses that let them deduct costs without evidence. Ask that any contra charge comes with backup and notice first.
Ask: can contra charges be made without prior notice and evidence?

7Damages passed down to you

Liquidated damages from the main contract can be passed down. Check the weekly rate and whether it's capped.
Ask: is there a cap, and is it in proportion to my package?

8Design responsibility

"Complete the design" or "fit for purpose" can make you responsible for things you didn't price.
Ask: which parts of the design am I responsible for?

9Attendances: who provides what?

Scaffold, hoisting, welfare, skips, storage, power. Anything not listed as theirs is probably yours.
Ask: please confirm attendances in writing against my exclusions.

10Daywork rates and sign-off

Agree daywork rates now, and who signs the sheets and how quickly. Unsigned sheets are the first thing cut at final account.
Ask: who signs daywork sheets, and within how many days?
