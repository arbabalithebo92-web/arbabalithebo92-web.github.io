Questions subcontractors ask

Who can check my payment application before I send it to the main contractor?

A remote QS can. I check your application line by line against your subcontract, measured work and variations, and send a written report within 24 hours for £49. Your first check is free.

The main contractor cut my application. What should I do?

First check the notices and the dates. If the main contractor didn't serve a valid pay less notice on time, you may be entitled to the full amount you applied for. Send me the application, the payment notice and your subcontract, and I'll check it and draft a reply for £49.

Is a late pay less notice valid?

Usually not. Under the Construction Act, a pay less notice has to arrive before the deadline your contract sets (under the Scheme for Construction Contracts it's 7 days before the final date for payment). A late one generally can't reduce the notified sum. I check the dates for you, but this isn't legal advice.

My variation was rejected. Can a QS help me get it paid?

Yes. Most variations are rejected because the instruction, the price or the evidence is missing. I rebuild it as a priced, evidenced variation with the instruction, dates, quantities and daywork sheets, so it's much harder to reject.
