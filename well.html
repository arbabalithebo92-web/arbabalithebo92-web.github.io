How it works

Mean dia = inner dia + wall thickness. Volume = π × mean dia × thickness × height. Dry mortar ≈ 30% of brickwork volume; 1 cement bag = 50 kg = 0.0347 m³. Bricks per m³ depend on brick size and joint; check your local brick.
