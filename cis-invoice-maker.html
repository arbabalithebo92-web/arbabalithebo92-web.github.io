Quick rules this tool follows

  • CIS rate: 20% if you're registered for CIS, 30% if you're not, 0% with gross payment status. The contractor deducts it and pays it to HMRC for you.
  • CIS applies to labour only, not to materials you bought for the job, and not to VAT.
  • Domestic reverse charge: if you're VAT registered and your customer is a VAT and CIS registered contractor (not the end user), you don't charge VAT. The invoice shows the VAT amount and says the customer must pay it to HMRC.
  • End user or intermediary: if the customer has told you in writing they are an end user, charge VAT as normal.
  • Your UTR goes on the invoice so the contractor can verify you with HMRC.

General guidance only, not tax advice. Check HMRC guidance or your accountant if you're not sure which rules apply to a job.
