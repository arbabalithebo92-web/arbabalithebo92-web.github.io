Good to know

• Since the Construction Act amendments (1 Oct 2011), release of a subcontractor's retention generally can't be made conditional on events in another contract (e.g. the main contract's certificate). If your subcontract has no release date, check the JCT/NEC terms or the Scheme for Construction Contracts.
• Once the release date passes, the retention should come through the payment cycle like any other sum due: chase it with a payment application.
• *Interest shown uses the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act rate of base rate + 8% a year, for illustration. Your contract may set its own rate. This tool is guidance, not legal advice.
