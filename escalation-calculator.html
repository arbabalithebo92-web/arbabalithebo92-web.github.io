How the formula works

Pn = a + b·(Ln/Lo) + c·(Mn/Mo) + d·(Sn/So) + … where a + b + c + d + … = 1.
Escalation for the IPC = V × (Pn − 1). A positive result is payable to the contractor; a negative result is a deduction (de-escalation).
Always use the weightages, sources and base dates in your own contract's Appendix-C / Particular Conditions. Check whether your contract has a ceiling, a threshold, or excludes variations and dayworks.
