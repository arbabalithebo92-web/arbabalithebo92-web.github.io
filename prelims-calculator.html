Why split them?

A flat percentage hides the one thing that drives most preliminaries: time. Site staff, welfare, security, plant and temporary services cost the same every week, whatever the contract value. Pricing them weekly gives you a clear cost per week of delay, which is exactly what you need to support an extension-of-time or loss-and-expense claim.
