Apna Ghar Program: zaroori baatein

• Loan: 25 lakh se 1 crore tak, 20 saal ke liye.
• Markup: pehle 10 saal fixed 5%, phir market rate.
• Ghar: 10 marla tak (5 aur 7 marla bhi), ya 1,500 sq ft tak flat.
• 90% loan, 10% apna hissa. Sirf pehla ghar (pehle se ghar na ho).
• Plot khareedna, ghar khareedna ya ghar banana, teeno ke liye.

Ye qist ka andaza hai (20 saal, 5%). Final shartein bank aur State Bank se confirm karein.

Bank BOQ kyun maangta hai?

Construction loan mein bank aksar ghar ka naqsha aur kharche ka andaza (estimate / BOQ) maangta hai, aur paisa marhalon mein deta hai. Sahi BOQ se aap ko pata hota hai ke har marhale par kitna cement, sariya aur paisa chahiye, aur thekedar zyada bill nahi bana sakta.

Rates: Lahore base andaza 2026 (grey 2,800 / 3,200 / 3,600 aur mukammal 4,800 / 6,400 / 8,600 Rs per sq ft). 1 marla = 225 sq ft. Ground floor 80%, upar 60%. Har sheher aur naqshe mein farq hota hai.
