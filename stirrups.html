How it is worked out

  • Count: stirrups are placed from the first one at the support face, at end spacing through each end zone and middle spacing between, with no gap bigger than the spacing.
  • Cutting length = 2 × (a + b) + 2 hooks of 10d − bend deductions (3 bends of 90° at 2d, 2 hooks of 135° at 3d), where a = B − 2 × cover − d and b = D − 2 × cover − d. Hook rules vary by code and drawing: follow your drawing.
  • Weight = d² ÷ 162 kg per metre. Check spacing and zones against the structural drawing.
