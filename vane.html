How it works

Both ends: Su = T / [πD²(H/2 + D/6)]. One end: Su = T / [πD²(H/2 + D/12)]. Field vane over-predicts Su in high-plasticity clays, so design value = μ·Su, with μ ≈ 1.7 − 0.54·log10(PI) (Bjerrum-type correction, capped at 1.0 for low PI). Check against your project's geotech report.
