How it works

  • Interest = late amount × (8% + reference rate) ÷ 365 × days late. Simple interest, not compounded.
  • Fixed compensation per invoice: £40 under £1,000, £70 from £1,000 to £9,999.99, £100 from £10,000.
  • Reference rate is fixed for each half-year: the base rate on 31 Dec (debts due Jan–Jun) or 30 Jun (debts due Jul–Dec).
  • Applies business-to-business, unless your contract gives its own "substantial remedy" for late payment.
  • In construction, the "final date for payment" comes from your contract or the Scheme. Miss a Pay Less Notice and the notified sum is due in full.

Guide only, not legal advice. Check your contract terms and get advice before issuing a claim.
