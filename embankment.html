How it works

Area = B·H + ½·n1·H² + ½·n2·H². Volume by average end area: (A1 + A2)/2 × L. Riprap = slope length H·√(1+n²) × thickness × L. Bulking factor converts compacted to loose volume (check your soil; typically 1.15–1.35).
